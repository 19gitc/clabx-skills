# Product Review Outline

Use this with `templates/specs/product-review.json`.

1. Cover - frame the decision and the period.
2. Stats - show the three product signals that changed.
3. Bottleneck - explain where friction moved.
4. Before/after - show the product change.
5. Segment table - separate user groups and decisions.
6. Timeline - propose next release sequence.
7. Quote/principle - make the decision memorable.
8. Closing - state the requested decision.
