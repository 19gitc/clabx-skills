# Course Module Outline

Use this with `templates/specs/course-module.json`.

1. Cover - lesson title and learner level.
2. Objectives - what learners can do by the end.
3. Problem - why the concept matters.
4. Misconception/model - contrast wrong and useful models.
5. Method - repeatable steps.
6. Practice - scenarios and feedback.
7. Closing - takeaway and next practice.
