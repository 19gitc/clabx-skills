# Venture Pitch Outline

Use this with `templates/specs/venture-pitch.json`.

1. Cover - literal category and offer.
2. Problem - why the current workflow fails.
3. Status quo - compare bad alternatives.
4. Solution - product wedge and core workflow.
5. Traction - real usage and commercial signal.
6. Market - buyer and expansion path.
7. Roadmap - why this becomes a platform.
8. Business model - how expansion works.
9. Raise - capital plan and milestones.
10. Closing - ask and next action.
