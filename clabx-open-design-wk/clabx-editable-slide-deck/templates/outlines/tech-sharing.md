# Tech Sharing Outline

Use this with `templates/specs/tech-sharing.json`.

1. Cover - system and technical claim.
2. Old path - what was coupled.
3. New shape - before/after architecture.
4. Tradeoffs - choices, benefits, costs.
5. Rollout - phased migration.
6. Success metrics - operational definition of done.
7. Principle - why this matters beyond cleanliness.
8. Closing - decision or next engineering step.
